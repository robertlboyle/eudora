The calendar

Click Calendar in the sidebar. Your calendars from Microsoft 365, Outlook.com, and Google are listed under it, each in its color; click one to show or hide it.

Views

Day, WeekThe hours of each day. With Business hours on, the business day fills the window so events are easy to read; events outside it are counted at the top or bottom of the day. Full day shows all 24 hours.
MonthThe whole month.
AgendaWhat's coming, as a list.
InvitesInvitations waiting for your answer. See Invitations.
TripsReservations found in your travel mail. See Trips and events from your mail.

A calendar's color: right-click a calendar in the sidebar and pick a color, or Another color… for any color. The calendar's own color goes back to the one Microsoft or Google shows. The color is Eudora's only: Outlook and Google keep theirs.

Double-click a day's heading in Week to open that day. Settings ▸ Calendar sets the first day of the week and the business day (7 AM to 7 PM at first).

Right-click an event

Open, Join the meeting, Quick Print; Accept, Tentative, Decline, Propose New Time (invitations); Reply, Reply All, Forward (a message to its people); Edit… and Send as .ics file… (meetings you organized); Private, Show As, Categorize (your labels, which are Outlook's categories); Delete.

Delete does what Outlook does: someone else's meeting is declined, and your own meeting with people is canceled and they're told. For a repeating meeting, choose This meeting or The whole series.

Canceled meetings leave by themselves: when someone else cancels a meeting that hasn't happened yet, Eudora takes it off your calendar (and off it in Microsoft 365 or Google) without sending anything, as Outlook does. Deleting a canceled meeting yourself removes it too: a canceled meeting can't be declined. Canceled meetings already over are left where they are.

Changes are made in Eudora at once and on the server in the background.

New appointments and meetings

Choose New in the calendar (or press N, or double-click an empty time in Day or Week). Fill in:

  • Title and Calendar (which of your calendars it goes on).
  • Start and End (moving the start keeps the length), or All day.
  • Location: places are suggested as you type. With your own Google Maps key (Settings ▸ Calendar) they come from Google Maps; without one, from OpenStreetMap. The place chosen has a Show on the map link.
  • Online meeting: Teams (a work Microsoft 365 calendar), Google Meet (a Google calendar), or Zoom. Only the kinds you have set up are offered.
  • People: with address completion. With people added, Save becomes Send invitations; Microsoft or Google sends them, and their answers come back into the event.
  • Show as, Reminder, Private, and Notes (formatted text).

Editing

Right-click a meeting you organized and choose Edit…. The people are sent the update. A Teams meeting already in it stays, and the notes are sent only if you changed them, so their formatting isn't lost.

Sending as an .ics file

Send as .ics file… attaches the event to a new message, for people who use another calendar.

Zoom

In Settings ▸ Calendar ▸ Zoom, type your Personal Meeting Room link: Online meeting ▸ Zoom then puts it in the appointment. For a new meeting of its own each time (its own ID and passcode), make a Zoom app as that page describes, type its Client ID and Secret, and choose Sign in to Zoom.

Invitations

Invitations you haven't answered are on your calendar (as tentative) and listed in the calendar's Invites view, a card for each with any meetings that conflict with it. The invitation email shows the same choices in a bar above the message:

  • Accept, Tentative, Decline: the organizer is told. Declining asks first. For a series, the answer covers every meeting in it.
  • Propose New Time…: Tentative or Decline with another start and end and a message.
  • Join, Show in calendar, Open the email.
  • Spam: for invitations sent as spam. Nothing is sent (an answer would tell the sender your address works); the meeting leaves your calendar quietly, the email goes to Spam, and later invitations from that sender are removed too.

An invitation to an account whose calendar Eudora doesn't manage (an IMAP account) is answered by email, as calendar programs answer each other; Eudora asks first, since it sends a message.

No answer buttons are offered where an answer can't be sent: for a calendar someone shared with you, and for a meeting whose organizer asked for no answers (there, Decline simply removes it from your calendar).

Trips and events from your mail

Eudora reads your travel mail from the last year for reservations still ahead:

  • Flights, trains, buses, ferries, and rides booked ahead are added to your calendar by themselves (when not already there), with the flight number, airports, times, and confirmation, a reminder 3 hours before, and the label Travel.
  • Hotel stays, Airbnb, and car rentals are offered, not added: 7 PM on the check-in day to 10 AM on the check-out day, shown as free.
  • Shows, concerts, movies, conferences, and trade shows bought through ticket sellers and registration services are added by themselves.

Eudora missed one? Right-click its email and choose Add to Trips.

The calendar's Trips view groups reservations into trips, named for where you're going, each with buttons: Add to calendar, Don't add, Show in calendar, Open the email.

Tickets travel with the event. Boarding passes, PDF tickets, Wallet passes, and QR or bar codes in the booking email, or in a later email about the same trip, are attached to the calendar event (on Microsoft calendars), so you have them on your phone when you open the event.

Contacts (Address Book)

Click Contacts in the sidebar or on the toolbar, or choose Tools ▸ Address Book (Ctrl+L). Your address books from Microsoft 365, Outlook.com, and Google are shown together. It opens at once with what Eudora last had, then catches up.

  • Find: type any part of a name, address, or company. Choose an account to see only its contacts.
  • Sort by first name, last name, or company.
  • Click a contact to see everything about it, and where it came from: the account, the address book, and whether it's up to date with the server.
  • Click an address to write to it.
  • Edit, New Contact, and Delete… (asks first): the change is made on the server too.
  • Export vCard… and Export CSV… (Outlook's and Google's column names) save the contacts shown.

To Do and flagged messages

Click To Do in the sidebar. Your tasks are kept in Microsoft To Do through your first Microsoft account, so Outlook, the To Do app on your phone, and Eudora on your other computers share one list.

  • Add: type in the box at the top; choose a due date (today, tomorrow, next week) if you like.
  • Tasks are grouped: Overdue, Today, This week, Later, Flagged messages, Someday, and Done (last month).
  • Tick a task to mark it done; ★ marks it important. Right-click for Edit… (title, due date, notes), due dates, Send to Twos, and Delete….

Showing or hiding lists

Open the arrow beside To Do in the sidebar to see every list: your own, Flagged email, and lists other people shared with you in Microsoft To Do (a colleague's or a shared mailbox's). Click a list to show or hide its tasks: its color is filled in when it's shown, an outline when it's hidden. Each task shows its list's color down its left edge. Right-click a list to pick its color (or Another color…, or back to its first color), as for a calendar. Lists shared with you start hidden, so only your own tasks show until you choose others. Hiding a list changes only what Eudora shows: Microsoft To Do keeps it and its tasks.

Flagging a message

Flag any message as a To Do with Message ▸ Flag as To Do (Ctrl+Shift+G), the Insert key, or right-click. It shows a ⚑ in the list, and a task for it appears under Flagged messages; ✉ Open Email on the task opens it so you can act on it. A Microsoft 365 message gets Outlook's flag too. Marking the task done, here or in Outlook, takes the flag off.

A flagged email's task has ✉ Open Email. Eudora finds the message by its Message-ID, which stays the same wherever the message is filed. When the message isn't found (deleted, or not downloaded to this computer), ✉ Find Email searches your mail for it.

Twos

If you use the Twos app, type your Twos user id and token in Settings ▸ To Do and choose Test…. Then Send to Twos puts a task on today's list there. Twos has no official connection for other programs, so this may stop working if Twos changes.
