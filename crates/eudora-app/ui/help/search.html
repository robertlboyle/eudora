Searching your mail

Type in the search box at the top of the window (Edit ▸ Find Messages…, Ctrl+F) and press Enter. Eudora searches every account, every folder, and every year at once (except mailboxes you left out of search): subjects, senders and recipients, the text of messages, the names of attachments, and the text inside PDF attachments of receipts and payments.

Results come newest first. Click a column heading to sort them another way. You can work with search results as with any list: read, reply, move, delete, or run the spam filter or your rules on them.

The last word you type also matches longer words starting with it: walkerfi finds walkerfield.example.

When nothing matches, Eudora says No results. When your search has a mistake, Eudora repairs what it safely can, says what it did, and offers corrected searches under Try:. Click one to run it.

To find words in the message you're reading or writing, use Find Text.

Search words and operators

Words and phrases

budget reportMessages with both words, anywhere.
"budget report"The exact phrase.
budget OR forecastEither word.
budget -draft or budget NOT draftLeave out messages with "draft".
budg*Any word starting with "budg".
(budget OR forecast) -draftParentheses group parts of a search.

AND is allowed but not needed: every word must match anyway.

Dates

today, yesterday, this week, last week, this month, last month, this year, last year, a year (2019), a date (3/15/2024 or 2024-03-15), a range (2023-01-01..2023-06-30), or before / after (>2024-01-01, <=2010). Weeks start on Sunday.

Sizes

tiny (under 10 KB), small, medium, large, very large, huge (1 to 5 MB), enormous (over 5 MB), or numbers: >5MB, 1MB..2MB.

Headers

Any word followed by a colon that isn't one of the words above searches the header of that name: x-mailer:eudora, list-id:* (has the header at all), -list-id:* (doesn't). While Eudora is still indexing headers of older mail, the results say how much it covers so far.

These are the words Outlook and Gmail use, so what you know from them works here too.

Find Text in a message

Edit ▸ Find Text… (Ctrl+Alt+F; in Classic mode Ctrl+Shift+F) finds words in the message you're writing, or else in the one you're reading, and highlights each. F3 finds the next, Shift+F3 the one before.

Leaving mailboxes out of search

Each row on the left (Accounts, Shared Mailboxes, every account, every folder) has a small magnifying glass beside its icon. Click it to leave that mailbox out of search: the magnifying glass is then struck through in red. Click it again to search it again.

A switch applies to everything under it, and a switch lower down wins. So you can leave out all of Shared Mailboxes, then switch one shared mailbox back on, or leave out a whole account and search just its Inbox.

Nothing is deleted or taken out of the index: the mail is still kept and still found the moment you search it again. A message that's also in a folder that is searched still shows.

While anything is left out, a magnifying glass with ! appears beside the search box. It's gray while searches leave those mailboxes out; click it to Include all mailboxes (it turns bold and colored), and again to leave them out. When a search finds more in the mailboxes left out, the results say how many, with Include them.
