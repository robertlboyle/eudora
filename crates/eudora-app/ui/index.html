Plugins

Plugins add commands to Eudora. Each runs sealed off inside Eudora: it can't reach your files, the network or other programs, only the parts of your mail you allow it, and everything it does is logged (Settings ▸ Programs and plugins).

Plugins aren't signed yet: Eudora can't check who made one. Install only plugins from people you trust.

Empty Trash

    
The messages in each Trash you tick are deleted. In Microsoft 365 mailboxes they're deleted on the server too, as Outlook's Empty Folder does: Exchange keeps them in Recoverable Items for a while (usually 14 to 30 days), where Outlook's "Recover deleted items" finds them. This can't be undone in Eudora.

What's new

    

    

    
Speed

    
How long things take on this computer: the usual time (half are quicker), the slow ones (95 of 100 are quicker), and the slowest ever. Kept as Eudora goes, at no cost to its speed.

    

    

  

  
    
Statistics

    
Counting…

    

  

  
    
Out

    
Messages waiting to be sent. Eudora sends each at its time while it's open (or its download is running); one whose time passed while both were closed goes at the next start.

    

    

  

  
    
Find Old Attachments

    
Classic Eudora kept attachments as files apart from the messages. Eudora looks for the ones still missing in your old Eudora folder, in other copies of the same messages (from Microsoft 365), and in a folder you name here: an old backup drive, an unzipped backup… Whatever it finds is kept in the library from then on (and in backups). Look as often as you like: only what's still missing is looked for.

    

      
    

    

    

  

  
    
Import

    
Brings mail in from files: a classic Eudora folder (its mailboxes, folders and settings), mailbox files (classic .mbx, mbox from Thunderbird, Apple Mail and others), or messages (.eml) and folders of them. Nothing is changed where they are. Messages already here aren't added twice; copies of the same message from elsewhere are merged.

    

      
      
      
      
    

    

    

  

  
    
Optimize Library

    
Gives back the disk space of deleted mail (Empty Trash), merges the search index and tidies the database. Every message moved is checked against its checksum first; nothing is lost. Downloading pauses meanwhile. On a big library this takes minutes.

    
    

    

  


  
    
Restore Eudora

    
Restores a backup (made on any computer: Windows, Mac or Linux) as Eudora's library. It goes into Eudora's usual place unless you choose another folder (another drive with more room, say); the folder must be new or empty, and outside Dropbox, OneDrive and other sync folders. Whichever folder it is becomes the library Eudora opens from then on.

Recent Mail

      
Recent Mail, at the top of the mailboxes, lists what the spam filter and your rules sorted out of the Inbox lately (marketing, notifications, mailing lists), never spam, so nothing new goes unseen.

      
      
    

    

      
To Do

      
Tasks are kept in Microsoft To Do (your first Microsoft account), so Outlook, the To Do app and Eudora on your other computers share them. Flag any message as a To Do with Ctrl+Shift+G (or right-click ▸ Flag as To Do).

      
Twos has no official API: Eudora uses the one its own apps use, which may change. Find your user id and token in Twos in a browser (its developer tools ▸ Network, in a request's payload).

      
      
      

      

    

    

      
Calendar

      
      
      
Day and Week show the business day filling the window (Business hours in the calendar's bar); Full day shows all 24 hours. Events outside it are counted at the top or bottom of each day.

      
Places for appointments

      
A new appointment's Location suggests places as you type: Google Maps' with your own Google Maps key (made in your Google Cloud project: Places API (New); Google bills its owner, with a free monthly allowance), else OpenStreetMap's (no key needed).

      
      

      

      
Zoom

      
Your Personal Meeting Room (zoom.us ▸ Profile ▸ Personal Meeting ID): Online meeting ▸ Zoom puts it in the appointment.

      
      
      
      

      
A meeting of its own for each appointment (its own ID and passcode) needs your Zoom app: zoom.us ▸ App Marketplace ▸ Develop ▸ Build App ▸ General App (user-managed, OAuth); scope meeting:write:meeting; redirect URL and allow list http://localhost:47891/zoom. Type its Client ID and Client Secret here, then Sign in to Zoom. Signed in, Online meeting ▸ Zoom makes a new meeting each time; otherwise it uses the room above.

      
      
      

      

    

    

      
Speed

      
Eudora can keep more of its database in memory, so counts, sorting and folders come back faster. It takes the memory only while Eudora is open.

      
      

      
Maintenance

      
Maintenance keeps Eudora quick: it checks every folder's counts, gives back the space of deleted mail, merges the search index, refreshes the database's statistics and counts Help ▸ Statistics. It runs in the background; the status bar shows each step.

      
      
      
      
      

      

    

    

      
Downloading

      
While Eudora is open it keeps your mail up to date by itself, in the background: new mail every 5 minutes (and at once with Check Mail), then older mail. Closing Eudora stops it; it carries on where it was next time. These take effect at once.

Not used for 15 minutes, a computer checks Gmail only this often; use Eudora again and it checks at once. Other accounts keep their own interval. More…

Updates

      
Eudora looks on software.planet.net for a newer version, downloads it in the background and checks its signature, then asks before installing it. Looking sends nothing about you: only Eudora's name and version. Help ▸ Check for Updates looks at any time.

      
      
      

      

    

    

      
Programs and plugins

      
Other programs and plugins can use your mail only as far as you allow them, one permission at a time, on this computer only. Everything they do is logged here.

      
      
      

      

      

    

    

      
Labels

      
Labels mark messages by color (Message ▸ Change Label). In Microsoft 365 mailboxes they're Outlook's categories, with the same names, so Outlook and your phone show them too. The list travels to your other computers.

      

      
 

    

    

      
Address completion

      
As you type in To, Cc or Bcc, Eudora suggests people from your mail.

      
      
    

    

      
Pictures from the web

      
Pictures in a message that load from the web can tell the sender you opened it. Eudora fetches them itself; the message never goes online.

      
Eudora always asks first (“Show pictures” in the message). Senders you trust can be marked with “Always show from …”: only their pictures load by themselves.
